Self-trained Gaussian Splatting

COLMAP + a 3DGS trainer on a free Colab GPU — maximum control.

Power option
In one line: do the Gaussian-splat training yourself on a free Colab GPU (or your laptop) from a normal video. Most control and the highest ceiling on quality — but the most technical.

COLMAP + a 3DGS trainer (gsplat / Brush / Nerfstudio) run on a Colab GPU; you tune the quality.

How it works, step by step

  • Record a steady phone video of the room and split it into frames.
  • COLMAP (structure-from-motion) recovers where each frame's camera was.
  • A 3D Gaussian Splatting trainer (gsplat, Brush, or Nerfstudio's gsplat) optimises the splat for a few thousand iterations on a free Colab T4 GPU.
  • Export the trained .ply splat.
  • SuperSplat clean-up → our web viewer → host.

Trade-offs

✓ Strengths

  • Highest possible quality — you control everything
  • Works from any video; no special app
  • Free on Colab; a real, defensible pipeline
  • Great to describe in the Technical Abstract

✗ Weaknesses

  • Most technical & time-consuming option
  • COLMAP can fail on feature-poor / blurry video
  • ~20–40 min per scene on Colab
  • Local run is tight on a 4 GB GPU (Colab avoids this)
