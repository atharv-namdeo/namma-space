Verdict for the competition
Go with Option 1 (cloud Gaussian splatting via Luma or Polycam) for the Round-1 visual: photoreal, reliable, almost no compute on your side. Keep Option 5 (monomap) as the safety net and as the navigation layer for Round 2 (it already outputs a floor-plan and occupancy map). Everything downstream — the SuperSplat clean-up and our web viewer — is identical no matter which option makes the splat.
